# Audio credits

The sound effects used by Windows Whip were downloaded from [Pixabay — whip sound effects](https://pixabay.com/ru/sound-effects/search/%D0%BA%D0%BD%D1%83%D1%82/).

They are provided under the Pixabay Content License, not the application's MIT license. The local pack contains one impact and two swings. For this application, the recordings are trimmed, level-adjusted, and converted to mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 48 kHz.

Sounds are embedded in the finished executable as part of the interactive application. Original MP3s and separate edited WAVs are not redistributed in this source repository.
