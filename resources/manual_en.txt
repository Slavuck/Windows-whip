Your cursor now has a whip. You're officially in charge of your desktop.

Move the mouse fast, then stop sharply. The whip strikes and makes a left click. Your normal mouse buttons still work.

Exact — the tip hits the point where your cursor stops. For those who came for results.

Sadomaso — the handle follows your cursor, while the tip moves with its own momentum. Feel like a true ruler: improve your aim with every strike.

F8 — take a break, or get back to teaching your desktop a lesson.

Escape — close the app. You can also choose Exit in the tray menu. In full-screen apps, the whip automatically takes a break.

Note: no matter how hard you strike, it won't make your apps run faster.

Note two: works best with dark-themed apps.
